Modules (syllabus outline)

  • Intelligent Agents, Environments, and Problem Solving through uninformed, Informed, and Adversarial Search
  • Constraint Satisfaction Problems (CSP) and Game Theory including Alpha-Beta Pruning
  • Knowledge Representation and Reasoning using First-Order Logic: Syntax, Semantics, and Inference (Forward/Backward Chaining, Unification, and Resolution)
  • Planning and Reasoning under Uncertainty, Probabilistic Reasoning, and Bayesian Belief Networks
  • Machine Learning Foundations — Linear and Logistic Regression, Gradient Descent, and Concept Learning
  • Supervised Learning via Decision Trees (ID3), Inductive Bias, and Support Vector Machines (SVM)
  • Unsupervised Learning involving Clustering, Mixture of Gaussians, and Principal Component Analysis (PCA)
  • Neural Networks, Ensemble Methods, and the EM Algorithm
  • Reinforcement Learning tasks including Markov Decision Processes, Q-Learning, and SARSA
  • Deep Learning and Transformer Architectures — Self-Attention Mechanisms, Multi-Head Attention, and Encoder-Decoder Frameworks
  • Large Language Models (LLMs) — Tokenization, Pre-training, Fine-tuning, and Prompt Engineering
  • Natural Language Processing through Formal Grammars, Syntactic/Semantic Analysis, and Machine Translation
  • Advanced Computational Paradigms — Expert Systems, Fuzzy Logic, Genetic Algorithms, and Swarm Intelligence
